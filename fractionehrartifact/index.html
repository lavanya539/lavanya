Can providers access previous notes during an encounter?

−

Yes. Existing medical history, medications, allergies, and previous encounter notes are available while recording the current visit, so providers don't need to switch between records.

What information can be documented in a clinical note?

+

A clinical note can include the chief complaint, history of present illness, review of systems, physical exam findings, assessment, diagnoses, treatment plan, orders, prescriptions, referrals, and follow‑up instructions.

Do orders and prescriptions stay connected to the encounter?

+

Yes. Lab orders, imaging requests, and prescriptions created during the visit are linked to the clinical note and the patient chart, making it easy to track what was ordered and why.

Can notes be compared across visits?

+

Yes. Previous encounter notes remain available for reference when documenting a new visit, so providers can review progress, compare findings, and build on prior assessments.

Is there a consistent note structure across all providers?

+

Yes. Documentation follows a structured format so information is organized predictably regardless of which provider completed the note, making it easier to review the record over time.

Does the clinical note connect to billing?

+

Yes. Diagnoses and procedures documented in the clinical note flow into the billing workflow, reducing the need to re‑enter encounter information and supporting accurate claim submission.

What types of orders can be created in FractionEHR?

−

Laboratory, imaging, diagnostic, and clinical orders can be created from the patient chart, each linked to the patient, the visit, associated diagnosis, and supporting notes.

Where do results and reports get stored?

+

Results and reports are attached directly to the patient record. Providers can upload or document results against the original order, making them part of the patient's ongoing chart.

Can providers review previous orders during a visit?

+

Yes. Order history is available in the patient chart during any encounter, so providers can see what was previously ordered, when, and what the results were before placing new orders.

How are follow‑up actions tracked after a result?

+

FractionEHR keeps follow‑up actions connected to the original order. Whether the next step is patient communication, a treatment change, another order, or a follow‑up appointment, it stays linked to the result in the patient record.

Are orders connected to the clinical encounter?

+

Yes. Each order is linked to the patient and the specific visit during which it was placed, along with the associated diagnosis, date, and supporting notes.

Can different team members access order history?

+

Yes. Order history is part of the patient chart, which is accessible to authorized team members based on role‑based permissions set during account configuration.

How is access assigned in FractionEHR?

−

Access is assigned by job function. Providers, front‑desk, billing, and admin teams each receive access to the tools and records required for their role — nothing more by default.

Can different staff roles see different parts of the system?

+

Yes. Providers see clinical records and orders; front‑desk staff see scheduling and registration; billing teams see claims and account balances; administrators have access to user management, settings, and reporting.

Who can adjust user permissions?

+

Administrators can adjust user permissions in FractionEHR. They can update access levels as staff join the practice, change roles, or leave — keeping permissions aligned with current responsibilities.

What happens when a staff member changes roles?

+

An administrator updates that user's permissions to reflect their new responsibilities. Previous access is removed and new access is assigned, keeping the system aligned with each person's current role.

Can access be restricted to specific patient records?

+

FractionEHR uses role‑based permissions to control which modules and functions each user can access. Administrators configure access based on job function so each user sees only what their role requires.

Does role‑based access apply to all FractionEHR modules?

+

Yes. Role‑based permissions apply across patient records, scheduling, clinical notes, billing, reporting, and all other modules. Every function in FractionEHR respects the access levels configured for each user's role.

About FractionEHR

Built on a proven foundation, managed for independent practices

FractionEHR extends the OpenEMR‑based foundation into a managed EHR and practice management platform. An FBS product, engineered by Hamly Globaltech, it combines OpenEMR’s flexibility with managed hosting, implementation support, billing workflow guidance, and dedicated assistance.

Focused on real‑world practice operations, FractionEHR is built for independent practices and clinics that need a complete, connected workflow without the burden of managing their own servers or infrastructure.
